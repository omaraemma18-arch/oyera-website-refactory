Who We Are

Oyera Auto Service Bay Ltd

OAS Bay is an auto service bay that handles heavy, small, and commercial cars. When your vehicle arrives, a senior technician assesses what parts and oils are needed for the job.

We stock items for sale at the bay — including filters, engine oil, and gearbox oil — so you can purchase what you need for service. One or more technicians may work on a single car during a service session.

  • Senior technician assessment on arrival
  • Filters, engine oil & gearbox oil available on site
  • Flat labour fee per service session
  • Heavy, small & commercial vehicles welcome

Our Process

How Service Works at OAS Bay

01

Vehicle Arrives

Bring in your heavy, small, or commercial vehicle. A senior technician reviews what service is required.

02

Assessment

The senior technician determines which parts and oils are needed — filters, engine oil, gearbox oil, and more.

03

Purchase Parts

You buy the required items at the bay. We keep filters, engine oil, and gearbox oil in stock for sale.

04

Service & Labour

Your car is serviced in the bay. Labour is charged as a flat fee per session; one or more technicians may work on the vehicle.
